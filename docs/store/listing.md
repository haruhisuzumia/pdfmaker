## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Panna (पन्ना, “page”) turns your phone into a free document scanner and PDF toolkit, made for everyday Indian paperwork: forms, ID cards, marksheets, agreements and portal uploads.

**Free. No ads. No watermark. No account.**
Every feature is free, your documents are never stamped with a logo, and you don’t need to sign up.

**Private by default**
Your scans stay on your phone in the Documents/Panna folder. Panna has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Google Drive backup is optional, and Panna can only see the files it creates in your Drive.

**Scan**
• Automatic edge detection and perspective correction
• Filters and cleanup to remove shadows and stains
• Multi-page scanning; reorder, rotate and delete pages before saving
• ID card mode: Aadhaar, PAN or driving licence, front and back on one A4 page at real size

**PDF tools**
• Compress to size: fit portal limits like 50 KB, 100 KB or 200 KB
• Merge PDFs, split PDFs, extract pages
• Edit pages of any PDF (reorder, rotate, delete, add scans)
• Images to PDF and PDF to images (JPGs in your Gallery)
• Sign PDFs with your own signature
• Extract text from scans in English and Hindi, all on your phone

**Passport and exam photos**
Make passport, visa, exam-form photos and signature images at the exact size and under the KB limit your form asks for.

**Works offline**
Scanning, PDF tools and text recognition run on your phone, even without internet.

**Optional Google Drive backup**
Keep a copy of your documents in your own Google Drive, upload on Wi-Fi only, and see everything in one list.

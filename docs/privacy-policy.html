Panna – Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · App: Panna – PDF Scanner & Tools (Android, io.github.haruhisuzumia.panna)

In short
  • Your documents stay on your phone. We have no servers and never receive your files.
  • No account is needed. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Google Drive backup is optional. If you turn it on, files go to your own Drive, and the app can only see files it created.

Who we are

Panna is an independent, free app made by haruhisuzumia. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Panna and “you” means the person using the app.

Data we collect

None. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data or documents. The app has no backend server, no advertising SDK, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Data stored on your phone

  • Documents you scan, import, create or edit are saved in the Documents/Panna folder on your phone. Images you export are saved in Pictures/Panna. You can see and delete these files with any file manager.
  • App data (the document list, page previews, saved signatures and your settings) is kept in the app’s private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Scanning and text recognition

Scanning uses Google’s ML Kit document scanner, and “Extract text” uses Google’s ML Kit text recognition. Both are provided by Google Play services and process images on your device. Google Play services may download the scanner and language models the first time you use them. These features are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. The app does not request the camera permission itself; the camera is opened by Google’s scanner.

Choosing photos uses Android’s system photo picker, so the app only receives the photos you pick and does not need permission to read your gallery.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose Connect Google Drive, you sign in with Google and allow two permissions:

  • drive.file: lets the app create, update, read and delete only the files it created in a “Panna” folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • userinfo.email: lets the app show which Google account is connected.

Your files travel directly between your phone and Google Drive over an encrypted connection; they never pass through any server of ours. Your email address and Drive access are kept only on your phone. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Disconnect Google Drive, which revokes the app’s access, or from your Google Account. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them. Documents you delete in the app are moved to your Drive’s Bin.

Panna’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the backup feature you turned on. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models.

Sharing

Documents leave your phone only when you choose to share them (for example with WhatsApp or email) or when you turn on Google Drive backup. Shared files are then handled by the app or service you chose.

Children

Panna is a general productivity app. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your documents are protected by Android’s storage security. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone or to the Documents/Panna folder can open your documents, so keep your phone locked.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
